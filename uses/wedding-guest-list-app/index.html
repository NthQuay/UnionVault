Families, Couples and Plus-Ones

Group guests into households — couples, families with children, or solo invitees with a plus-one. The list shows each household as a collapsible row, so you can see your numbers without the clutter.

Add children and infants with their own age bracket. Mark plus-ones as named or unnamed and convert them to a real guest once the name comes in.

A-List and B-List Invites

Tag each household as A-list or B-list. Send your A-list invites first, then promote B-list guests as declines come back. A progress bar shows how many invites have been sent versus how many are still waiting.

Mark all A-list invites as sent in one tap. Filter by sent, unsent, A-list or B-list to see exactly where things stand.

Dietary and Contact Details

Record each guest's dietary requirements — vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut allergy or a custom note. Dietary icons appear on the seating chart so your caterer and venue can plan ahead.

Store phone numbers, email addresses and postal addresses. Use them to send RSVP nudges, email invitations or print postal labels for your thank-you cards.

Import and Bulk Actions

  • Import guests from a CSV or Excel spreadsheet
  • Bulk-assign tags, dietary flags or invite status
  • Search and filter by name, tag, RSVP status or table
  • Export the full guest list to CSV for your venue or caterer
  • Running headcount of adults, children and infants
