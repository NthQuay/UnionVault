Minute by Minute

Add each moment of your wedding day to the timeline — ceremony start, speeches, first dance, cake cutting, bouquet toss. Set the time, location and who is responsible.

Drag items to reorder, adjust times and let the rest of the schedule shift automatically. Add buffer time between moments so nothing feels rushed.

Music Cues

Attach a song to any moment on the timeline. Your DJ or musician sees exactly which track plays when — processional, recessional, first dance, cake cutting, entrance song.

Add the artist, song title and any notes (like "start at the chorus" or "fade out after 30 seconds"). Your music list lives alongside the run sheet so nothing is missed.

Share with Your Team

Generate a shareable link to the run sheet. Your bridal party, photographer, videographer, celebrant and venue coordinator each see the full schedule on their phone — no login required.

Update the timeline and everyone with the link sees the latest version. No more printing new copies or forwarding updated PDFs.

All Connected Together

  • Timeline items appear on your planning calendar
  • Link vendors to timeline moments so their contact details are one tap away
  • Your wedding website shows the day-of schedule to guests
  • Reorder, duplicate or delete moments with drag and drop
  • Works offline — plan on the go and sync later
