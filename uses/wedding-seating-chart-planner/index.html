Visual Floorplan Builder

Build your reception layout on a zoomable canvas. Choose from round, square, long or theatre-style tables, then drag them into position. Resize, rotate and snap to a grid.

Add venue elements like a dance floor, stage, bar, entrance, cake table, photo booth or buffet station. Label zones with text so your vendors know the plan.

Drag and Drop Guests

Your unassigned guest list sits beside the floorplan. Drag a name onto a table to seat them, or drag between tables to reassign. Couples and families stay grouped.

Use the list view for a table-by-table overview, or switch to the floorplan to see exactly where everyone sits. A banner counts how many attending guests still need a seat.

Conflict Detection

Mark guests who should be kept apart. If they end up at the same table — or even neighbouring tables — you get a warning. Split couples and separated family members are flagged too.

Dismiss a conflict if you have decided it is fine. Bring it back later if you change your mind.

Export and Print

  • Export to PDF, PNG image or CSV spreadsheet
  • One-page or two-page layout (floorplan plus guest list)
  • Black-and-white mode for clean printing
  • Show or hide seat counts on the final plan
  • Dietary icons (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, allergy) on every seat
