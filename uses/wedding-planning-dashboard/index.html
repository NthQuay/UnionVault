Dashboard

Wedding Planning Dashboard

Wedding planning dashboard that puts your guest counts, budget, checklist progress and next tasks on one screen the moment you sign in. Customise which widgets show, reorder them to suit you, and print or export whenever you need a shareable copy.

See your dashboard — it's free

Everything at a Glance

See invited, attending, declined and awaiting guest counts, your checklist completion percentage, and your total budget the moment you open the app — no digging through separate pages.

A "Next Up" list surfaces the tasks that actually need attention first — due dates within the month take priority, then undated and pinned tasks, then anything further out. A separate list flags budget payments due in the next 31 days, overdue ones included.

Customise Your Layout

Switch to Edit Layout and drag widgets into the order that makes sense for your planning — Stats, Planning Progress, RSVP Summary, Budget, Budget at a Glance, your pinned event countdown, Vendors, Vision Board and Wishing Well.

Hide any widget you don't need yet, and some widgets can sit side by side while others — like Stats and Wishing Well — always take the full row. Your layout is saved and syncs across your devices.

Shortcuts to Every Tool

Each widget links straight through to its full page — tap your Vision Board preview to open the whole board, your Wishing Well progress to see every contribution, or your budget glance to open the full tracker.

  • Choose exactly which Vision Board ideas show in the widget, not just the first few
  • Vendor lookup keeps upcoming appointments and bookings one tap away
  • A countdown to your wedding day stays pinned for quick reference

Print or Export Anytime

Open the Print / Export hub from your dashboard to generate shareable documents — guest lists, budgets, timelines and more — without leaving the page you're on.
