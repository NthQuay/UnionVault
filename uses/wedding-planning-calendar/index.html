Everything on One Screen

The calendar pulls in dates from across your wedding plan automatically. Checklist deadlines, vendor deposit due dates, RSVP-by dates, appointments and additional celebrations all appear in a single view.

Colour-coded dots distinguish task types at a glance — blue for checklist items, gold for payments, green for events. Tap any dot to see the full detail and jump to that section of the app.

Month and Week Views

Month view gives you the big picture — see which weeks are busy and which have breathing room. Week view zooms in with a day-by-day breakdown and more detail per item.

Swipe between months or tap a date to jump straight there. The calendar always opens to the current week so the most urgent items are front and centre.

No Manual Entry

You never add dates to the calendar directly — they flow in from the tools you are already using. Set a due date on a checklist task and it appears on the calendar. Add a vendor payment and the due date shows up automatically.

This means the calendar is always accurate without any extra work. One source of truth, zero duplication.

Stay on Track

  • Overdue items highlighted in red so nothing slips through
  • Wedding countdown shows how many days until the big day
  • Filter the calendar by category — tasks only, payments only, events only
  • Additional celebrations (rehearsal dinner, welcome drinks) have their own date entries
  • Works offline — check your schedule anywhere
