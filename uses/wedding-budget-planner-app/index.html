Wedding Budget Planner App

Wedding budget planner app that tracks deposits, payments and due dates in one place. Set your total budget, break it into categories and always know what you have committed, paid and still owe.

Your Budget at a Glance

A four-figure summary shows what you have committed, what you have paid, what is left to pay and how much is still unallocated. If you go over budget the numbers turn red.

Choose from four display styles — stat cards, a progress bar, icon rows or a donut chart — and collapse the panel when you want more room.

Categories and Deposits

Twelve pre-built categories cover venue, florals, catering, photographer, attire, rings, hair and makeup, entertainment, transport, stationery, honeymoon and miscellaneous. Add sub-items within each one.

Track the deposit amount, deposit due date, balance due date and whether each has been paid. Set up instalment plans on individual line items and mark each payment as it goes through.

Per-Head Pricing

Flag any line item as per-head and it multiplies automatically by the number of confirmed guests. Link it to your RSVP count so the total updates as responses come in.

Optionally exclude children from the per-head calculation for items like bar packages or adult-only catering tiers.

Due Date Alerts

  • Overdue deposits and balances flagged in a pinned alert bar
  • Upcoming payments highlighted before they are due
  • Sidebar badge shows overdue (red) and due-soon (amber) counts
  • View everything still owed, sorted by due date, in one summary
  • Reports for committed breakdown, payment timeline and invoices
