RSVPs

Online Wedding RSVP Tracker

Online wedding RSVP tracker that gives your guests a simple form to respond on, then organises every reply in one place. See who is coming, who has declined and who still needs a nudge.

Share a Link or QR Code

Your guests receive a link or scan a QR code to reach your RSVP form. No app download, no login. They tap Attending or Declined, choose a meal, and submit.

Customise which fields guests fill in — phone number, email, postal address, song request or a free-text comment. Mark each one as required or optional.

Meal Choices and Headcounts

Turn on meal selection and set your menu options. Guests choose for themselves when they RSVP. A separate children's menu handles the little ones.

Your dashboard shows confirmed adults, children and infants at a glance. A donut chart or progress bar gives you the response rate and a breakdown of attending, declined and still awaiting.

Nudge Guests Who Haven't Replied

Set an RSVP-by deadline and see how many days are left. For guests who still haven't responded, send a friendly nudge by pre-filled text message or email — one tap per guest, or send a bulk email to everyone with an address on file.

Override the deadline for individual guests when you need to. The tracker shows exactly who is outstanding.

Invites, A-List and B-List

Track which households have been sent an invite and which have not. Invites group automatically — one per couple, one per family.

  • Separate A-list and B-list so you can send in waves
  • Mark all A-list invites as sent in one tap
  • Invite progress bar with a sent-vs-unsent breakdown
  • Rearrange your RSVP dashboard with drag-and-drop widgets
