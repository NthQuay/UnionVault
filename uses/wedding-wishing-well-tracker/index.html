Wishing Well

Wedding Wishing Well Tracker

Wedding wishing well tracker that lets you set a cash gift goal, log every contribution, and keep thank-you notes on track. Share your payment details with guests and watch the progress bar fill up.

Set Your Goal and Payment Details

Choose a dollar amount and write a personal message that your guests will see on your wedding website. Add your payment details — PayID, BSB and account number, or PayPal — so guests know exactly where to send their gift.

Set suggested amounts (like $50, $100, or $200) to make it easy for guests to choose. Your wishing well page shows your message, payment options and a live progress bar.

Track Every Contribution

Log each gift as it arrives. Record the contributor's name, amount, date, payment method and an optional note. Sort by newest, highest amount, or alphabetically.

A progress bar tracks how much has been raised against your goal, with a percentage and contributor count. You always know exactly where you stand.

Never Miss a Thank-You

Each contribution shows whether a thank-you has been sent. Mark them off as you go, and check how many are still due at a glance.

If a contributor's name matches someone on your guest list, their postal address is pulled in automatically. Print postal labels for your thank-you cards directly from the tracker.

Share with Your Guests

Your wishing well has its own section on your Union Vault wedding website. Guests see your message, suggested amounts and payment options — without seeing who has contributed or how much.

  • Add links to external gift registries alongside the cash wishing well
  • Track physical gifts received and who they came from
  • Preview exactly what guests see before your site goes live
