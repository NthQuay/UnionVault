Tasks Linked to Budget

Each checklist item can link to a budget line item. When you tick a task like "Pay florist deposit", the payment amount and date log in your budget tracker at the same time. No double entry.

See which tasks still have an unpaid cost attached. A badge on the checklist icon shows how many overdue payments need attention.

Timeline Categories

Tasks are grouped by how far out they fall — 12 months, 9 months, 6 months, 3 months, 1 month, 1 week and on the day. Expand or collapse any group to focus on what matters now.

Add your own tasks, reorder them, and set custom due dates. The pre-loaded checklist covers the essentials but every wedding is different.

Alerts and Progress

An alert bar pins to the top of the checklist whenever tasks are overdue or due within the next seven days. Overdue items show in red, upcoming items in amber.

A progress bar tracks how many tasks you have completed against the total. Archive finished tasks to keep the active list clean, and bring them back if you need to.

Everything in One Place

  • Checklist tasks appear on the planning calendar alongside vendor payments and events
  • Completed tasks feed the dashboard progress widget
  • Filter by category, status or due-date range
  • Add notes and attachments to any task
  • Works offline — ticks sync when you reconnect
