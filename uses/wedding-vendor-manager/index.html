All Your Vendors in One Place

Add each vendor by category — florist, photographer, videographer, caterer, celebrant, DJ, hair and makeup, transport, cake and more. Store their business name, contact person, phone, email and website.

Open any vendor card to see their full payment history, upcoming appointments and notes. Search and filter by category or status.

Payment Timeline

Each vendor has a payment timeline showing deposits, instalments and the final balance. See what has been paid, what is due next and the total still outstanding.

Payments link to your budget tracker, so marking a payment as paid in either place keeps both in sync. Overdue amounts are flagged in red across the app.

Shareable Vendor Briefs

Generate a vendor brief link for each supplier. It shows them the event details, timeline, guest count and any notes you have added — without exposing your budget, other vendors or private information.

Your photographer sees the schedule and locations. Your caterer sees the headcount and dietary breakdown. Each brief is tailored to what that vendor needs.

Appointments and Reminders

  • Schedule vendor meetings and tastings with date, time and location
  • Appointments appear on the planning calendar
  • Suggested appointment types per vendor category (e.g. tasting for caterer, trial for hair and makeup)
  • Add notes and attachments to any vendor — contracts, mood boards, quotes
  • Vendor count and total spend summary on the dashboard
