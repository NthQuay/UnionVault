Vision Board

Wedding Vision Board

Wedding vision board that turns the photos you save into a beautiful moodboard. Arrange them scrapbook-style, as a clean grid, magazine rows or a mixed collage, then pin your favourites to your dashboard.

Build yours — it's free

From Inspiration to Board

Save photos to your Inspiration library as you find them — venues, florals, dresses, cakes, anything that catches your eye. Mark your favourites and they appear on your Vision Board automatically.

Drag cards to reorder them exactly how you want. Toggle captions on or off, or switch to images only for a cleaner look.

Four Layouts to Choose From

Scrapbook pins your photos at a tilt with tape and botanical line-art, like a page from a wedding scrapbook. Grid lines everything up into even, aligned tiles. Editorial lays photos out in magazine-style rows. Mood Board mixes card sizes into a collage.

Switch between them any time — your photos and their order carry over. Choose X-Small, Small, Medium or Large cards to fit more on screen or make each photo stand out.

Build Your Colour Palette

Pick swatches from a curated wedding colour library, or add your own custom shades. Your palette sits alongside your board so every vendor conversation starts from the same page.

Pin Favourites to Your Dashboard

Add your Vision Board to your dashboard and choose exactly which ideas show there — not just the first few. Pick as many or as few as you like, any time.

  • Show images only on the dashboard for a tidier widget
  • Change your pinned selection whenever your ideas evolve
  • Everything on the board stays private to you and your partner
