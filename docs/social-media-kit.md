## Video Content Guide

### Recommended tools

| Tool | Cost | Best for | Platform |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| **Canva** | Free tier (Pro has more templates) | Static posts, carousels, story templates, simple Reels with text overlays | All platforms |
| **CapCut** | Free | Video editing, trending templates, auto-captions, transitions | TikTok + Reels |
| **InShot** | Free (premium removes watermark) | Quick mobile edits — trim, crop, add music, text | Reels + Stories |
| **Instagram Reels editor** | Free (built-in) | Simple cuts, text overlays, trending audio, filters | Instagram only |
| **DaVinci Resolve** | Free | Professional-grade video editing on desktop (steeper learning curve) | All platforms |

### Recommended workflow

1. **Screen-record the app** on your phone (iOS: swipe down from top-right, tap screen record; Android: pull down notification shade, tap screen record)
2. **Edit in CapCut** — trim dead time, add text overlays, pick trending audio, enable auto-captions
3. **Export at 1080x1920** (9:16 vertical)
4. **Post natively** on each platform (don't cross-post with watermarks — TikTok watermarks hurt Instagram reach and vice versa)

### Video content ideas

**Quick wins (can make right now):**
- Screen recording walkthrough of each feature (30-60 seconds each)
- "Setting up your wedding in 60 seconds" — speed-run through sign-up, add guest list, enable RSVPs
- Before/after: messy spreadsheet vs clean Union Vault dashboard (split screen)

**Engagement-driven:**
- "3 things I wish I knew about wedding budgets" (talking head + app screenshots)
- "How to avoid the seating chart nightmare" (tips + app demo)
- "The one thing 90% of couples forget on their RSVP form" (hook + reveal)
- "POV: You just got engaged and need to plan a wedding" (relatable + app solution)

**Trending formats:**
- "Things that just make sense" — list Union Vault features over trending audio
- "Tell me you are planning a wedding without telling me" — duet/stitch format
- "Wedding planning hack" — quick tip with app demo

### Best practices

- **Vertical 9:16** for Reels, TikTok and Stories (1080x1920px)
- **15-30 seconds** for highest completion rate and algorithm boost
- **Hook in first 2 seconds** — start with a question, bold claim or visual surprise
- **Add captions** — 80% of social video is watched on mute. CapCut auto-captions are the fastest way
- **Post consistently** — 3-4 times per week beats daily burnout. Batch-create a week of content in one session
- **Use trending audio** — check Reels and TikTok "trending" tabs weekly. Even a 5-second clip of trending audio helps reach
- **Repurpose across platforms** — same content works on Reels, TikTok and Facebook. Just re-export without watermarks for each
